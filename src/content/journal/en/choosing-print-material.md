A great photo deserves a great print. Here is an overview of the four options in my [print shop](/en/shop).

## Fine art paper

Hahnemühle Photo Rag is a matte cotton paper with a subtle texture. Black and white images gain incredible depth. **Best for:** street photography and collectors who frame themselves.

## Aluminium Dibond

The image is mounted on an aluminium composite panel – borderless, modern and durable. **Best for:** strong colours, landscapes and modern spaces.

## Framed with mat

The classic: fine art print behind anti-reflective glass with a mat in a black wooden frame. **Best for:** gifts and living rooms.

## Canvas

Stretched on a frame, matte and warm. **Best for:** large formats at a fair price.

## Which size?

Rule of thumb: the image should cover about two thirds of the width of the furniture below. Above a 2-metre sofa, 120 × 80 cm looks great.
