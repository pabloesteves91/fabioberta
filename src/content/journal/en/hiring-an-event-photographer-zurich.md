An event lasts a few hours – its images stay with you for years. Whether it is a company anniversary, a championship or a club gathering, the right preparation gets the most out of your event photographer.

## 1. Define what the images are for

Social media, press release, annual report or participant gallery? Each purpose needs different formats. Tell me upfront and I will plan vertical shots for Instagram as well as calm horizontals for your website.

## 2. Share the schedule early

A simple timeline with the key moments – welcome, keynote, award ceremony – is all I need to be in the right place.

## 3. Name the key people

CEO, sponsors, speakers, athletes: a short list (ideally with photos) makes sure nobody is missed.

## 4. Think about light

Many venues in Zurich are dark with mixed lighting. I work with fast lenses and subtle flash, but a quick check of the stage lighting always helps.

## 5. Plan delivery time

You will receive a first selection within 48 hours – faster on request.

## 6. Clarify usage rights

May sponsors use the images? Will they appear in advertising? I state this transparently in my quote.

## 7. Choose someone with an eye for emotion

Great event photos are about genuine moments – something I learned in street photography.
