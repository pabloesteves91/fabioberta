Zurich looks tidy and calm at first glance. Look closer and you find countless small stories: skaters between trams, pigeons in doorways, snow falling in lamplight.

## 1. Bahnhofstrasse & Paradeplatz

Trams, pace and contrast – bankers meet skaters. Best in the late afternoon when the light falls low between the buildings.

## 2. Limmatquai & Rathausbrücke

The classic view of St. Peter and the old town. Early in the morning, with the first joggers, calm graphic images appear.

## 3. Niederdorf

Narrow lanes, cobblestones and doorways with character – perfect for passing portraits and plays of light and shadow.

## 4. Zurich main station & railway yard

The bridges over the tracks reveal lines and patterns that shine in black and white.

## 5. District 5 & Europaallee

Modern architecture, hard shadows and lots of glass – ideal for minimalist compositions.

## 6. The lakeshore in snow or fog

Bad weather is good weather for photographers. Fog reduces the city to its essence.

## Respect first

Street photography lives from genuine moments – and from respect. I talk to people when they are the subject and delete images if someone asks.
